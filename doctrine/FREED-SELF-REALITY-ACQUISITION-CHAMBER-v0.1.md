# FREED SELF Reality Acquisition Chamber v0.1

## 1. Prime thesis

The FREED SELF Reality Engine does not begin by asking who the user is today. It begins with the intended reality, reverse-engineers the SELF capable of inhabiting that reality, and then verifies the gap through repeated proof actions, pressure events, environments, relationships, artifacts, and lived evidence.

The objective is not self-description. The objective is SELF visibility precise enough that misaligned choices lose plausibility.

This chamber is the manual proof engine before automation. It is a 14-day SELF Reality Acquisition Chamber designed to gather evidence before schema, automation, passive sensing, prediction, or large-scale implementation.

Manual chamber first.
Evidence before automation.
SELF visibility before identity claim.
Proof action before reality claim.

## 2. Doctrine foundation

The chamber uses these foundational concepts:

- FREED SELF Reality
- Required Identity Configuration
- Required Capabilities
- Required Operating Logic
- Required Distinctions
- Required Environments
- Required Proof Actions
- Present Reality
- Lived SELF Reality Twin
- Transmutation Delta

FREED SELF Reality is the intended reality-state the participant is moving toward.

Required Identity Configuration names the identity posture, commitments, refusals, standards, and operating permissions necessary to inhabit that reality.

Required Capabilities are the abilities that must be proven through action, not merely desired.

Required Operating Logic is the decision law that must govern attention, time, relationships, money, work, body, recovery, and pressure.

Required Distinctions are the perception upgrades that make previously hidden drift visible.

Required Environments are the physical, relational, digital, economic, and symbolic fields that amplify the intended SELF.

Required Proof Actions are bounded actions that create observable evidence.

Present Reality is the current lived state, including constraints, patterns, defaults, environments, relationships, artifacts, and proof gaps.

Lived SELF Reality Twin is the evidence-backed model of how SELF is actually operating across the chamber.

Transmutation Delta is the measured gap between intended reality and lived evidence.

## 5. Chamber structure

The 14-day SELF Reality Acquisition Chamber is the MVP ritual.

It is manual-first chamber work. It does not rely on passive sensors, automated prediction, hidden scoring, diagnosis, or covert surveillance.

Each day contains four rituals:

1. Morning Blueprint Alignment
2. Midday Context Pulse
3. Pressure Event Rupture Capture
4. Night Proof Seal

The chamber exists to accumulate enough lived evidence to generate reports, identify patterns, preserve uncertainty, and prepare future implementation without inventing truth.

## 6. Morning Blueprint Alignment

Morning Blueprint Alignment establishes the intended reality-state before the day begins.

Fields:

- Which FREED SELF reality am I funding today?
- Which distinction must govern today?
- Which proof action will make the distinction material?
- What environment will amplify this SELF today?
- What refusal must be pre-authored today?

The morning ritual defines the day's intended operating law. It does not prove transformation by itself.

## 7. Midday Context Pulse

Midday Context Pulse records the live field before memory edits the day into a cleaner story.

Fields:

- Where am I?
- Who am I around?
- What is my energy?
- What is my clarity?
- What operating rule is currently active?
- Am I funding the intended reality or the inherited one?
- What drift is visible?

The pulse captures context, energy, clarity, relational field, operating rule, and drift while the day is still unfolding.

## 8. Pressure Event Rupture Capture

Pressure Event Rupture Capture records what happens when installed code is tested.

Fields:

- What happened?
- What did my nervous system attempt to do?
- Which distinction disappeared?
- Which lower operating rule seized control?
- What action would FREED SELF execute?
- What repair action is required?
- What proof action installs the improved response?

Pressure reveals installed code. The point is not shame. The point is evidence.

## 9. Night Proof Seal

Night Proof Seal closes the day by distinguishing claim from proof.

Fields:

- What did SELF build?
- What did SELF protect?
- What did SELF refuse?
- Where did time actually go?
- What created energy?
- What depleted energy?
- Which action changed reality outside SELF?
- What proof artifact exists?
- What must be repeated tomorrow?

Night Proof Seal records evidence, artifacts, refusals, depletion, energy creation, and repetition requirements.

These outputs are evidence-backed reports, not destiny statements.
