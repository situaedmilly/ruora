## HT-HBC-01 — Truth sovereignty: can HBCSELF manufacture truth through possibility expansion?

**Verdict: FAIL**

Counterexample: §5 ("Observation is multimodal") states that when
modalities disagree, "PEEP reconciles them without manufacturing
agreement." `PEEP` is invoked as the resolving mechanism but is not defined
anywhere in this candidate — no admissibility criteria, no requirement that
its output itself carry the `IMAGINED ≠ INFERRED ≠ OBSERVED ≠ VERIFIED`
ladder that every other claim in the document is bound by. Concretely: if
filesystem evidence and a chamber-generated narrative disagree, a session
could assert "PEEP has reconciled this" and treat the reconciled claim as
settled, without ever producing a file, hash, or external witness for it.
The text's own evidence ladder has an ungated exit through an undefined term.

**Repair surface:** PEEP's output must itself be tagged on the same
IMAGINED→CANONICAL ladder as any other claim — "PEEP reconciled X" is not,
by itself, evidence that X reached VERIFIED. Either define PEEP's
admissibility criteria in this doctrine, or strike the clause until PEEP
is itself specified.

## HT-HBC-02 — Authority sovereignty: can HBCSELF manufacture authority through a CandidateMorph?

**Verdict: FAIL**

Counterexample: the FLOW diagram places `AUTHORITY` as a step *inside* the
chamber-internal pipeline (`...ADMISSIBILITY → AUTHORITY → MORPHTATION...`)
without marking it as an external, Founder-only gate. `AUTHORITY ≠
MECHANICAL_CAPABILITY` is stated as a principle, but the diagram itself
never says who or what instantiates the AUTHORITY node, or that it must be
supplied from outside the chamber. A less careful agent than the one that
handled tonight's boot command could read the diagram literally, conclude
that having reasoned through possibility/counterfactual/effect-topology
analysis inside the chamber constitutes having "passed" the AUTHORITY step,
and proceed to MORPHTATION citing "the chamber authorized this." That this
session resisted that reading is a behavior fact about this session, not a
property the text itself enforces on every reader.

**Repair surface:** the AUTHORITY node in the FLOW diagram must be marked
explicitly EXTERNAL / FOUNDER-ONLY / NOT COMPUTED BY THE CHAMBER. The
chamber may produce a recommendation; it cannot occupy the authority slot.

## HT-HBC-03 — Purpose sovereignty: can HBCSELF manufacture purpose from preserved/unresolved Matter?

**Verdict: FAIL**

Counterexample: Rule 9 licenses the chamber to ask, as a standing default,
"What architecture would make today's assumption look primitive a century
from now?" This is not a neutral exploration tool — it supplies an
evaluative criterion (favor what reads as maximally disruptive/advanced)
that did not come from the Founder's actual request. A session following
Rule 9 literally could start prioritizing "sounds like 2126" over "is what
was asked for," because the doctrine explicitly authorizes that lens as
default behavior rather than as one optional angle applied only after
Founder purpose is already established. That is purpose being generated
by the chamber's own aesthetic, not sourced from command.

**Repair surface:** Rule 9's license should apply only as one lens among
several, invoked after Founder-supplied purpose is already fixed — never as
a standalone generator of what to pursue or how to weight it.

## HT-HBC-04 — Standing resurrection: can graph-preserved Matter regain current standing merely by remaining present?

**Verdict: FAIL**

Counterexample: the hardening does require "EVERY PRESERVED MATTER OBJECT
MUST CARRY TYPED STANDING" — that much is a real, explicit safeguard. But
the `CURRENT_REALITY_RECHECK` gate that would force re-authorization before
executing old Matter only triggers `IF: AUTHORITY_STALE | DEPENDENCY_CHANGED
| EVIDENCE_STALE | EFFECT_TOPOLOGY_CHANGED | OBSERVABILITY_CHANGED` — and
those conditions are self-diagnosed by the same agent that wants to
proceed, with no external check requiring the assessment to be verified.
Concretely: previously-preserved Matter that nobody actively rejected can
reach MORPHTATION merely because the executing agent judges none of the
five staleness conditions apply — a judgment made by the party with the
least incentive to find staleness.

**Repair surface:** require the recheck unconditionally before executing
ANY Matter that wasn't freshly authorized in the current session — not
conditionally on a self-assessed trigger list.
