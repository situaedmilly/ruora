## 3. AD-2 — THE TRICHOTOMY: EXPOSURE vs BINDING_NOVELTY

**Verdict: TWO IRREDUCIBLE EVENTS (reading a). Readings (b) and (c) KILLED (H-8).**

Shared-repo case: artifact X committed by SELF A into repo R, co-custodied by A and B.

- **At commit:** custody(A) and custody(B) both already hold (shared substrate). Exposure(A, authoring session) occurred at formation. Exposure(B) has NOT occurred. Binding(B) has NOT occurred. Binding(later-A-session) has NOT occurred — A must still situate its own representation against changed reality (G-1; the estate's memory-vs-repo reconciliation is this event, daily).
- **When B runs `git show`:** payload crosses B's cognitive surface — exposure(B) occurs, an institutionally adjudicable event (membrane jurisdiction). If and when B binds it against B's reality, situation(B) occurs (SELF jurisdiction).

Double dissociation:

1. **Exposure without binding:** B's session sweeps the file for an unrelated string, or loads it truncated — the payload transited B's surface without any binding attempt. Under blind-experiment stakes this divergence is load-bearing: the membrane is breached AT EXPOSURE. A blinded SELF whose context accidentally ingests the payload has its independence destroyed even if it never situates it.
2. **Binding without fresh exposure:** re-situation of an already-exposed representation after the binder's reality changed (v0.2 §10: re-attempt after reality change = new SituationOccurrence) requires no new exposure event.

Under reading (b) (one event, two views), exposure-without-binding is unrepresentable — a membrane breach with no situation is invisible, so the blind cannot be audited. KILLED. Under reading (c), if exposure is a predicate of binding, the same breach-invisibility kill; if binding is a predicate of exposure, re-binding-without-fresh-exposure becomes unrepresentable, killing v0.2 §10's own re-situation law. KILLED both directions.

**Relation between the two:** first-binding of alien matter requires prior/concurrent exposure; exposure never requires binding; re-binding requires neither. Consequence for R02's joint repair direction (H-5): index SITUATION to binding-novelty — yes; but do NOT re-index RECEIPT to binding-novelty, which would collapse S06 (Receipt != Situation). The missing middle term between custody and situation is EXPOSURE. Receipt becomes media-conditional; exposure becomes the universal crossing event.
