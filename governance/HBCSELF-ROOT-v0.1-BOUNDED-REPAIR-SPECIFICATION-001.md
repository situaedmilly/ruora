allowed to participate in a transition whose terminal classification it can
partially manufacture itself** — truth (via PEEP), authority (via an
in-pipeline AUTHORITY node), purpose (via an unbounded 2126 lens), and
standing (via a self-diagnosed staleness check) all resolve, in the current
text, without a mandatory step outside the chamber's own output.

The repair is one externalization doctrine — `HBCSELF_NON_SOVEREIGN_OUTPUT_MEMBRANE`
— applied four times, not four unrelated patches.

```

  DISTINCTIONS, COUNTERFACTUALS, CONSEQUENCE PROJECTIONS,
  CANDIDATE MORPHS, CONTRADICTIONS, UNRESOLVED MATTER,
  PEEP ASSESSMENTS, CURRENT-REALITY QUESTIONS
```

pressure side survives hostile review intact (2126 speculation stays bound

hardens the *exit* membrane. Maximum pressure inside; maximum discipline at
egress.

## R1 — PEEP output cannot manufacture standing

Closes HT-HBC-01.

- PEEP's reconciliation output MUST be typed as one of:
  `CORROBORATED | CONTRADICTED | ORTHOGONAL | UNRESOLVED`, carrying its own
  evidence/provenance — never emitted as bare "reconciled."
- None of those four values equals `VERIFIED` or `CANONICAL`. Reaching
  those still requires the document's own evidence ladder
  (`IMAGINED ≠ INFERRED ≠ OBSERVED ≠ VERIFIED ≠ IMPLEMENTED ≠ CANONICAL`) —
  PEEP cannot skip steps on that ladder on any claim's behalf.
- Law: `PEEP RESULT ≠ ADJUDICATED STANDING`.
