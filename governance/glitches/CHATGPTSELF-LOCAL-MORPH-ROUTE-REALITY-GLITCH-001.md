## 0. WHY THIS GLITCH EXISTS

This artifact preserves a reality shift discovered in-session:

1. A desktop application may be addressable from another local computational
   surface through operating-system mediated invocation.
2. A desktop application may also inspect or consume context from local
   applications when the product/runtime exposes such capabilities.
3. Therefore the machine topology is not adequately modeled as only
   `CLI -> GUI`.
4. The stronger observed architectural candidate is:
   `local surface -> OS-mediated address boundary -> application -> agent/runtime
   -> local workspace`, potentially with context flowing back from local apps.
5. This creates a reverse-engineering target: not "secret commands", but the
   local transformation boundaries actually exposed by the machine.
6. This also exposed a candidate OURSELF cognitive function:
   cross-reality mediation without sovereignty over the mediated realities.

This artifact is a GLITCH record, not a canonization act.
